Listen now

Choose a session and press play. Daily Start streams over your internet connection unless you have saved that session offline.

Your sessions, ready wherever you are

Download on Wi-Fi to listen without relying on a signal. In the Library, choose Energy, Focus, Confidence, Reset or the entire library, then hold to save. The full ten-session library is about 84 MB; the app shows how much is still needed and skips sessions already saved.

Save an individual session

Press and hold Save offline until the ring completes. You can also use Download options or the accessible Save offline action. Keep the app open until the download finishes. When it says Available offline, the session is ready without an internet connection.

Manage your downloads

You can cancel a download or a collection in progress, retry the remaining sessions, or remove a saved copy to free space. Cancelling a collection keeps completed downloads. Downloads use the same audio quality as streaming; the benefit is reliable access without an internet connection. Favorites and offline downloads are separate: a heart helps you find a session; a download saves its audio.

If playback stops

Check your connection and audio output, then retry. If you are offline, choose a fully downloaded session. Try a comfortable listening volume on your headphones or speakers.

Send useful feedback

Email soundpaw@hotmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, the session title and what happened. Include whether you were streaming or listening to a saved copy. Please leave out passwords and payment details.
