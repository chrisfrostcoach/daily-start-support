Account and membership in TestFlight

In account-enabled beta builds, open Account on Today to use Sign in with Apple. Listening research is optional and off by default. You can choose whether to share new listening summaries, export your account data or delete your account in Account & privacy.

Open Membership on Today to preview the plans. A build labelled Demo membership uses reference prices of US$9.99/month or US$59.99/year with a preview of the eligible seven-day annual trial. Confirming demo access does not charge you, start a real trial or create a subscription. Use Reset demo to try the flow again. Real Apple checkout will be identified separately when enabled.

Listen now

Choose a session and press play. Dawn and Reset are free; membership-enabled builds offer the full 14-session library through membership or clearly labelled demo access. Existing permanent Starter Collection rights remain valid in purchase-enabled builds. YES Audio streams in High quality AAC over your internet connection unless you have saved that session offline.

Your sessions, ready wherever you are

Download on Wi-Fi to listen without relying on a signal. In the Library, choose Energy, Focus, Confidence, Reset or the entire library, select a download quality, check the size, then hold to save. The full 14-session library is about 135 MB in High quality or 391 MB in Lossless. The app shows the remaining size for your selected quality and skips matching copies already saved.

Save an individual session

Choose the download quality in the session player, check the file size, then press and hold Save offline until the ring completes. You can also use Download options or the accessible Save offline action. Keep the app open until the download finishes. When it says Available offline, the session is ready without an internet connection.

Choose your audio quality

High quality saves the same AAC audio used for streaming. Lossless, where available, preserves the mastered audio without lossy compression in 16-bit, 48 kHz ALAC files. Choose it from Download quality in the player, Library or guide. This setting applies to future downloads; it does not upgrade an existing AAC copy or change streaming. Saved Lossless audio is preferred when you next press play.

For lossless listening, use compatible wired headphones or a wired audio setup. Most Bluetooth listening uses lossy compression. Choose a comfortable fit and volume; good earbuds, over-ear headphones and speakers can all work well.

Manage your downloads

You can cancel a download or collection in progress, retry remaining sessions, or remove either quality separately from the session’s download menu. Cancelling keeps completed downloads. Saving both qualities uses space for both files. Favorites and offline downloads are separate: a heart helps you find a session; a download saves its audio.

If playback stops

Check your connection and audio output, then retry. If you are offline, choose a fully downloaded session. Try a comfortable listening volume on your headphones or speakers.

Send useful feedback

Email soundpaw@hotmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, the session title and what happened. Include whether you were streaming or listening to a saved copy. Please leave out passwords and payment details.
